# AtliQ Hospitality Business Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents a **data-driven business analysis of AtliQ Grands**, a 5-star hotel chain operating across **Delhi, Mumbai, Hyderabad, and Bangalore**.

The objective of the analysis is to identify key business insights from hotel booking, revenue, occupancy, and customer-rating data and provide actionable recommendations to improve **revenue, occupancy, and customer satisfaction**.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze hotel performance across different cities and properties.
* Identify the properties and cities generating the highest revenue.
* Understand the relationship between **customer ratings, occupancy, and revenue**.
* Analyze occupancy patterns across weekdays and weekends.
* Evaluate the impact of room categories, booking platforms, and other variables.
* Identify underperforming properties requiring targeted strategies.
* Discover unusual trends that require further investigation.

## 🔍 Key Insights

* **AtliQ Exotica** is the most profitable property, generating approximately **0.32B in revenue**.
* **Mumbai** is the most profitable city.
* **AtliQ Blu** has the highest occupancy (**62%**) and the highest average customer rating (**3.96**), making it the strongest property in terms of customer satisfaction.
* **AtliQ Seasons** is the weakest-performing property, with approximately **0.06B revenue**, **44% occupancy**, and an average rating of **2.29**.
* **AtliQ Exotica Hyderabad** underperforms compared with other Exotica properties, with **45% occupancy** and a rating of **2.33**.
* Hotel rooms have higher occupancy on weekends, while customer feedback remains relatively consistent across days.
* **Revenue, occupancy, and average rating show a direct correlation**, suggesting that improving customer satisfaction can contribute to higher occupancy and revenue.
* Bookings involving **5 guests show an unexpected decline in average ratings**, requiring further investigation.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, AtliQ Grands should prioritize **customer satisfaction and service quality** as a key driver of business performance.

Particular attention should be given to:

1. Developing a targeted improvement strategy for **AtliQ Seasons**.
2. Investigating the performance issues at **AtliQ Exotica Hyderabad**.
3. Understanding the reason behind the lower ratings associated with **5-guest bookings**.
4. Using property- and city-level insights to improve occupancy and revenue.

## 🛠️ Analysis Areas

The project covers:

* Revenue Analysis
* Occupancy Analysis
* Customer Rating Analysis
* Property Performance
* City-wise Performance
* Booking Platform Analysis
* Room Category Analysis
* Weekend vs. Weekday Trends
* Dependency & Correlation Analysis
* Data Cleaning & Assumptions

## 📌 Data Assumptions & Cleaning

The analysis includes data-cleaning and analytical assumptions, including correcting the city information for a property whose city was incorrectly recorded. Occupancy was considered based on the check-in day/first day of stay, and revenue was calculated for one day of stay.

## 📈 Conclusion

The analysis indicates that **customer satisfaction is a critical factor for improving AtliQ Grands' overall business performance**. While properties such as AtliQ Exotica and AtliQ Blu demonstrate strong performance, AtliQ Seasons and AtliQ Exotica Hyderabad require focused improvement strategies.

This project demonstrates how **data analysis and visualization can be used to convert hotel operational data into actionable business insights**.
